Our Story

A Green Bay chiropractic clinic, on the same street, for 33 years.

Advantage Chiropractic Clinic of Green Bay opened in 1993 at 1804 South Ashland Avenue, and the clinic has cared for Northeast Wisconsin patients from that address ever since. Thirty-three years of continuous independent practice in a city that has watched chain franchises arrive, expand, and standardize the chiropractic experience.

The continuity matters. A patient who came in for a low-back consultation in 2003 can come back in 2026 and still be cared for under the same clinic name, by a team that has been working together long enough to share notes across cases. That kind of tenure is rare in independent chiropractic, and it is the foundation that lets the clinic offer a credential set most multi-doctor groups in the state cannot match in one address.

"Ten Best of The Bay wins in a row. A DABCI internist. A Webster-certified prenatal specialist. A four-technique chiropractic stack. All on South Ashland Avenue."

The clinic combines a DABCI diplomate (Dr. Cheryl Metzler), a Webster Technique-certified prenatal and pediatric doctor (Dr. Jon Swanson, also FMT and Rocktape), and a four-technique chiropractor (Dr. Alexander Kirchner: Gonstead, Diversified, Activator, and Cox Flexion Distraction). The community has noticed. The Press-Gazette readership has voted the clinic Best of The Bay ten years running, an unbroken streak in a category where ten years is a generation of practitioners.

How long has Advantage Chiropractic Clinic been open?
